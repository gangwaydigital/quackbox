# Emails

This is the main inbox view: a list of every message, with filters, a quick search box and a reading pane.

## Controls

- **Search box** at the top. Results appear as you type, and a small dropdown previews the top matches. Use the arrow keys to move through them and **Enter** on a highlighted preview to open it. Press **Enter** with nothing highlighted to remember the search; with the box empty and focused, your recent searches are listed, with **Clear all**.
- **Keyword / Smart** button beside the box. Keyword matches the words you type. Smart searches by meaning, so "contract renewal" can find an email that says "extending our agreement". Smart needs the embeddings that [Topics](/help/topics) creates.
- **Filters** panel (tap **Filters** on a phone): Sender, Mailbox, Date from, Date to, and Attachments (any, has, none). **Clear** resets them.
- **The list** shows sender, subject, a snippet, the date, a paperclip for attachments and a **thread** tag for conversations. Click a row to read it. Use **Prev** and **Next** under the list for more pages.

Searching clears the filters and choosing a filter clears the search. For both at once, use [Search](/help/search).

## Reading an email

Clicking a row opens the message beside the list. Press `j` and `k` (or the arrow keys) to move to the next or previous email, and `Esc` to close. Drag the divider to resize the pane. On a phone the email opens full screen.

At the top of the message:

- **Thread** opens the whole conversation.
- **Summarize** asks the AI for a short summary, action items and key points.
- **Similar** lists up to eight related emails with a match percentage (needs embeddings from [Topics](/help/topics)).
- **.eml** downloads the original message.

Under the header you can switch **Plain** and **HTML** (when the email has both) and use **Strip quotes** to hide the quoted text of earlier replies. Attachments are listed with a download link.

![An open email with the Summarize and Similar buttons](/help/email-detail.png)

## Example

You remember an invoice from a supplier last spring. Type the supplier's name, press **Enter**, then clear the search, pick **Date from** and **Date to** for March to May and **Attachments: Has attachments**. The list now shows only mail with files in that window.

> **Tip:** The open-in-full-page icon in the pane header gives you a bigger reading view you can bookmark.
