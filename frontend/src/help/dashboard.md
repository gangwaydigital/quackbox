# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the overview of your whole archive: how much mail you have, when it was sent, and who sent it.

## What you see

- **Stat cards** at the top: Total Emails, Unique Senders, Mailboxes, Threads and Date Range. The Unique Senders and Threads cards are links. If Threads shows a dash, open [Threads](/help/threads) and build them.
- **Email Volume**: a chart of emails per month, with a percentage compared to last month, and a strip showing the latest month, this year's total and the busiest month. Hover the chart for exact numbers.
- **Top Contacts**: the ten people you exchanged the most mail with, with a small volume bar. Click one to open their profile. **View all** opens [Contacts](/help/contacts).
- **Activity Patterns**: a heatmap of the days and hours when mail arrives.
- **Year by Year**: totals per year.
- **Thread Insights**: your longest threads, threads with the most people, and the longest running ones. **Browse threads** opens [Threads](/help/threads).
- **Quick actions** at the bottom jump to Search, Calendar, Attachments and Timeline.

![Dashboard with stat cards, volume chart and top contacts](/help/dashboard.png)

## Controls

- **Refresh** reloads all numbers. The Dashboard remembers its last result for five minutes so it opens instantly, and Refresh skips that memory.

## Example

You import five years of mail and want to know when the inbox was busiest. Look at the volume chart: the busiest month is named in the strip below it. Then open the Activity Patterns heatmap to see which weekday and hour is the peak.

> **Tip:** Numbers update after an import. If they look old, press **Refresh**.
