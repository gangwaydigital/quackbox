# Timeline

The Timeline is a scrolling list of milestones that QuackBox finds in your archive. It is a good way to see the story of a mailbox.

## What you see

Each card has a date and a short description, grouped under year markers. Milestone types:

- **First Email** and **Latest**
- **Busiest Days**
- **First Contact** with a frequent sender
- **Big Threads**
- **Volume Surge** and **Volume Drop**
- **Milestone** for round email counts such as the thousandth email

Cards can link to the email (**View email**), the conversation (**View thread**) or the sender (**View profile**).

## Controls

The filter chips at the top (**All** and one chip per type) show a single kind of milestone.

![Timeline of milestones with type filters](/help/timeline.png)

## Example

Click **Big Threads** to see the longest conversations in your archive, then click **View thread** on one to read it.

> **Tip:** The Timeline is calculated from your imported mail. It has nothing to edit, so the only way to change it is to import more.
