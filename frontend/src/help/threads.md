# Threads

Threads groups messages into conversations by following replies, so you can read a discussion from start to end.

## Before you start

If the page says "No conversations yet", click **Rebuild Threads**. QuackBox rebuilds conversations from the reply headers in your mail and shows progress. Do this again after importing a lot of new mail.

## Controls

- **Message count** menu: 2+, 3+, 5+ or 10+ messages, to hide short exchanges.
- **Sort** menu: Most Recent, Most Messages, Most People or Longest Running.
- **Rebuild Threads**.
- Click a row to expand it and see the messages as a tree. Each row shows the number of messages, participants, duration and dates. Click a message in the tree to read its text, and **View full email** to open it.

![Threads list sorted by most messages](/help/threads.png)

## A thread page

Open one with the **Thread** button on an email, or **View thread** on the [Timeline](/help/timeline). It lists every message in order. **Summarize Thread** asks the AI for a summary with **Key Decisions**, **Action Items** and **Open Questions**. Threads with five or more messages are summarized automatically when you open them.

## Example

To find the longest running conversations, set **Sort** to **Longest Running**, expand the first row, open one of its emails, click **Thread**, then **Summarize Thread** to catch up in a minute.

> **Tip:** The [Network](/help/network) graph is built from threads, so build them first.
