# Network

The Network is a graph of the people in your archive. Two people are connected when they appeared together in the same email conversations.

## Controls

- **Min shared threads** (1+, 2+, 3+, 5+, 10+): how many conversations two people must share to be connected. A higher number gives a cleaner graph.
- **Top** (40, 80, 120, 150): how many contacts to draw, starting with the busiest.
- Drag the background to pan and scroll to zoom. The **+**, **-** and reset buttons above the graph zoom in, zoom out and return to the start. The zoom level is shown between them.
- Hover a dot to see the person, their number of emails and connections. Their connections are highlighted. Click a dot to open their profile.
- Thicker lines mean more shared threads.

![Network graph of contacts who share threads](/help/network.png)

## Before you start

If the graph is empty, build threads first on [Threads](/help/threads), or lower **Min shared threads** to 1+.

## Example

To find who sits at the center of a project, set **Min shared threads** to 3+ and **Top** to 40. The dots with the most lines are the people everyone talks to.

> **Tip:** Large archives draw slowly with **Top** at 150. Start small.
