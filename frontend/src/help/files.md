# Files

Files is the index of attachments. It has three tabs.

## Browse

- **Search filenames** box and a **Sort** menu: By Date, By Name or By Type.
- Chips such as `.pdf 120` show the most common file types. Click one to filter by it.
- Each row shows the file name, the email subject and sender, the date and type. Click the name to open the email.
- The eye icon previews PDF and Word files, and the arrow downloads the file.
- **Zip N** downloads up to 500 of the attachments that match your search as a single zip, sorted into folders by month.

![Files list showing attachments and type chips](/help/files.png)

## On Disk

Apple Mail keeps many attachments in separate folders that do not link cleanly to one email. **On Disk** lists files found by walking those folders. You can search by filename, filter by mailbox and download. If it is empty, use **Scan now** on the Browse tab (it appears when Apple Mail attachments are detected).

## Content Search

Searches the text inside PDF and Word files, not just their names. QuackBox extracts the text after each import. Type your words and press **Enter** or **Search**. The refresh button re-indexes the attachments.

## Example

To find the contract that someone attached last year, open **Content Search**, type a phrase from inside it, and open the result to see the email it came with.

> **Tip:** Case search covers attachment text too, but only after it has been indexed. Run this once first.
