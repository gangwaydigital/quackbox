# Calendar

The Calendar shows how many emails arrived on each day, as a month grid. It is for spotting busy periods and jumping to a particular date.

## Controls

- The arrows beside the month name move to the previous or next month.
- Each day with mail shows its count and a shade of blue: the darker the blue, the more emails. The legend under the grid shows the scale (Less to More). Days without mail are grey and cannot be clicked.
- Click a day to open a panel below the grid listing that day's emails with sender, mailbox and time. Click an email to read it.

![Calendar month with the busiest days darker](/help/calendar.png)

## Example

You know a project went wrong around the end of a quarter. Open that month, look for the darkest days, and click the darkest one to read what was going on.

> **Tip:** The Calendar is about when emails were sent. For meeting invites with a start time, location and attendees, use [Events](/help/events).
