# Chat

Chat lets you ask questions about your archive in plain language. QuackBox looks up relevant emails, then your AI model writes an answer from them.

## Controls

- Type a question and press **Enter** (or the send button). Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
- Suggested questions on the empty screen are a quick start.
- The model button at the top right shows the model in use and lets you choose another one that your AI provider has installed.
- Under an answer, the **sources** are the emails it used. Click one to read it.

![Chat answering a question with source emails](/help/chat.png)

## Example

Ask `Who emailed me most about the studio move last year?`. Read the answer, then open the sources to check the details yourself.

> **Careful:** The model can be wrong or miss emails. Treat the answer as a pointer and verify against the source emails. For a careful, reviewable search use [Cases](/help/cases).
