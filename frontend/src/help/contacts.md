# Contacts

Contacts lists everyone who sent you mail, with tools to see how you talk to each of them and to merge duplicates. It has three tabs.

## Directory

- **Search contacts** matches a name or an email address.
- **Sort**: Most Emails, Most Recent, Name A-Z or Earliest Contact.
- **CSV** downloads the list.
- Each row shows the name, address, number of emails, threads and mailboxes. Click one to open the profile.

![Contacts directory sorted by most emails](/help/contacts.png)

A **profile** shows a relationship score with a breakdown, total emails, threads, first and last contact, volume over time, the hours and days when they email you, mailboxes, common subjects and recent emails.

## Sentiment

Sentiment shows how positive or negative the wording of your mail is, as five percentages from very positive to very negative, trends monthly or yearly (switch with **Monthly** and **Yearly**), the most positive and most negative senders, and how urgent the language is over time. **Refresh** recalculates.

> **Careful:** Sentiment counts positive and negative words from a built-in list. It runs instantly and needs no AI, but it cannot read sarcasm or context. Treat it as a rough guide.

## Merge

Merge finds people who appear under several names or addresses and lists each group with the reason it matched (same name, or similar address). **Merge** combines the group so all its mail shows under the address with the most emails. A confirmation appears first.

> **Careful:** Merging changes your database and cannot be undone.

## Example

To check whether one person is split across two addresses, open **Merge**, find their name, look at the addresses and counts, and click **Merge** only if they are really the same person.
